Golden Rose Assured Scheme (GRAS 12+1)

  1. Enrollment: You must duly fill in & sign the application form and deposit it at the Showroom with two photographs and a copy of any one identity card document[cite: 1]. Collect the Passbook and keep it in safe custody[cite: 1].
  2. Installment Amount: Monthly installment is in multiples of Rs. 1,000/- (Minimum Rs. 2,000/-)[cite: 1]. You can pay installment amounts in multiples of the scheme opted for Golden Rose Assured Scheme[cite: 1].
  3. Payment Mode: The first installment must be paid by cash, card, or UPI at our Showroom[cite: 1]. Subsequent monthly installment payments can be made through Cash / Cheques / Post Dated Cheques (PDC) / Demand Draft / ECS / Online UPI[cite: 1]. Always verify the entry in your Passbook at the cash counter[cite: 1].
  4. Cheque Policy: Only one cheque per account per month will be accepted[cite: 1]. No outstation cheques accepted[cite: 1]. In case of cheque dishonor, Rs. 350/- penalty will be charged per transaction[cite: 1].
  5. Monthly Due Date:
    • 10th of every month: For all accounts opened from the 1st to 15th of a month[cite: 1].
    • 25th of every month: For all accounts opened from the 16th to the end of a month[cite: 1].
  6. Continuous Payments & Extension: You must pay installments continuously for 12 months to be eligible for the 13th-month bonus[cite: 1]. If you miss any installment, the maturity date extends accordingly (maximum up to 3 skipped months)[cite: 1]. If missing more than 3 months, the scheme pre-matures without bonus eligibility[cite: 1].
  7. 12+1 Assured Bonus Structure: Suman Jewellers contributes the final installment as your bonus[cite: 1]:
    • Rs. 3,000/month × 12 = Rs. 36,000 + Rs. 3,000 (Bonus) = Rs. 39,000/- Jewellery Worth[cite: 2]
    • Rs. 5,000/month × 12 = Rs. 60,000 + Rs. 5,000 (Bonus) = Rs. 65,000/- Jewellery Worth[cite: 1, 2]
    • Rs. 10,000/month × 12 = Rs. 1,20,000 + Rs. 10,000 (Bonus) = Rs. 1,30,000/- Jewellery Worth[cite: 2]
  8. Delivery Pricing: The price of jewellery shall be at the actual market rate at the time of delivery upon maturity, not on the enrollment date[cite: 1].
